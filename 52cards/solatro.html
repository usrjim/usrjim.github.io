Solatro

Setup

  1. Standard 54-card deck (incl. 2 Jokers). Shuffle.
  2. Keep a score sheet: round number, bonus points, owned tokens.
  3. Round 1 target = 25. Each new round: +25 (R2=50, R3=75, …).

Rules

  • Each round: draw 8 cards, get 3 plays + 3 discards (discard 1–5 cards each time). Draw back to hand limit after each play/discard.
  • On a play, choose up to 5 cards to make a poker hand; score it (see chart), then set those cards aside until next round.
  • Jokers are wildcards. If drawn, bank it and draw a replacement. A banked Joker can be added to any future play. If a second Joker appears while one is banked, you lose BOTH.
  • After 3 plays, total your scores. Meet/beat the target → advance. Miss with no Shield → you lose.
  • Excess points (beyond target) spend at the Bonus Shop. You may own only one of each token; points don't carry between rounds.

Hand Scores

  • High card 5 · Pair 10 · Two pair 20 · Three of a kind 30 · Straight 40 · Flush 50 · Full house 60 · Four of a kind 100 · Straight flush 125 · Royal flush 150

Bonus Shop

  • Extra Card 10 · Extra Discard 25 · Pocket Points 50 · Extra Hand 75 · Double Score 100 · Shield 150
