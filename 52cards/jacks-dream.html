Jacks' Dream

Setup

  1. Pull the four Jacks into your hand.
  2. Remove 9s, 10s, Queens, Kings (20 cards).
  3. Shuffle the remaining 32 (Aces through 8s).
  4. Deal 5 columns face up, 6 cards each.
  5. Place the 2 leftover cards on top of columns 1 and 5.

Rules

  • Move a top card onto another of the same rank to form a stack; stacks move as a group.
  • A stack of all 4 of a rank becomes a pillow: flip one face down, set the other three aside.
  • Pillows only go onto a Jack or an empty column; pillows never stack on each other.
  • Jacks play from your hand anytime — onto a pillow or a column without a Jack. Anything may go onto a Jack; Jacks never return to hand.
  • Win: all numbers are pillows, and each Jack sits on its own column with a pillow below and a pillow above.
