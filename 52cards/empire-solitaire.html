Empire Solitaire

Setup

  1. Standard 52-card deck, but only 18 cards are used; set the other 34 aside.
  2. Deal 15 cards face up in 3 rows of 5 (non-overlapping).
  3. Place the remaining 3 cards face down as a stock pile.

Rules

  • Goal: collapse the board into a single stack.
  • A card may conquer another card of the same rank or same suit:
    • Any adjacent card (including diagonally), OR
    • Any card in the same row/column with no cards between (a rook move).
  • The conquered card is removed from play (or stays under the conqueror as part of one stack — your choice of variant).
  • You may turn over a stock card at any time, but it must be placed face-up on an empty board space before any other move. You can't flip a stock card as your first move (no empty spaces yet).
  • All 3 stock cards must be played during the game.
  • Win when all cards form a single stack (or one card remains, in the removal variant).
