Kings in the Corner

Setup

  1. Shuffle a standard 52-card deck. Deal 7 cards face down to each player.
  2. Place 4 cards face up under the tabbed stalls (one per stall). If a King shows up, leave it there until a player moves it during play.
  3. Place the remaining cards face down over the chip pot — the draw deck.

Rules

  • Goal: be first to empty your hand each round; first to 100 points wins the game.
  • Cards build down in alternate colors (K Q J 10 … A low) in the tabbed stalls; suit is irrelevant.
  • Kings stalls: must be started with a King; cards there can't be moved.
  • You may move an entire stack from one tab stall to another if the bottom card is playable on the target. Open tab stalls can be filled from your hand.
  • If you're dealt or draw a King, you must place it in an open Kings stall immediately (penalty for holding one). You may also move a King from a tab stall to a Kings stall.
  • On your turn: make as many plays as you wish (not forced, except Kings). If you make no play, pay 1 chip to the pot. End by drawing one card from the deck (penalty for forgetting). A drawn King must be played immediately (no redraw).
  • When a player goes out, every other player pays 1 chip per card left in hand. The winner takes the pot and scores those chips (1 chip = 1 point). First to 100 points wins.
