Card Capture

Setup

  1. Pull all 2s, 3s, 4s, and both Jokers — shuffle as your Personal Draw Deck.
  2. Shuffle the remaining 44 cards = the Enemy Draw Deck (place on your left).
  3. Draw 4 enemy cards face up into Positions 1–4 (left to right, away from the deck).
  4. If any J/Q/K/A appear, move them to the bottom of the Enemy Deck; refill later.

Rules

  • Goal: capture all enemy combatants (Aces + face cards) using non-face cards.
  • Each round has 4 phases:
    1. Enemy — slide cards right to fill empty positions, then draw replacements.
    2. Discard — you may discard any number of cards from your hand.
    3. Draw — draw from your Personal Deck until you hold 4 cards. If empty, reshuffle your discard pile into a new Personal Deck.
    4. Capture — do one of:
      • Capture: spend same-suit cards from hand totaling ≥ the enemy card's value. Spent + captured cards go to your discard.
      • Let enemy capture: Position 1 enemy card + a card from your hand go to the Enemy Capture Pile.
      • Sacrifice: discard 2 hand cards to send an enemy row card to the bottom of the Enemy Deck.
  • Jokers act as any one card in your hand.
  • Values: J=11, Q=12, K=13, A=14.
  • You lose immediately if any A/J/Q/K ends up in the Enemy Capture Pile.
  • Win when the Enemy Deck and all 4 Positions are empty and no face card was ever captured.
