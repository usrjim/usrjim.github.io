Regicide

Setup

  1. Castle deck: shuffle 4 Kings face down, 4 Queens on top, 4 Jacks on top. Flip the top card face up = the current enemy.
  2. Tavern deck: shuffle 2s–10s + 4 Aces (Animal Companions). Set the 2 Jesters aside (not in the deck).
  3. Draw 8 cards into your hand.

Rules

  • Goal: defeat all 12 enemies (Jacks → Queens → Kings). Win when the last King falls.
  • Enemy stats: Jack 10 atk / 20 HP · Queen 15/30 · King 20/40.
  • Each turn has 4 steps:
    1. Play a card (attack = its number; A=1) or yield.
    2. Activate the suit power (mandatory):
      • ♥ Heal — shuffle discard, count cards = attack value, tuck them under the Tavern deck.
      • ♦ Draw — draw cards = attack value (capped by max hand size).
      • ♣ Double — damage = 2× attack value.
      • ♠ Shield — reduce enemy attack by attack value (cumulative).
    3. Deal damage; if total ≥ enemy HP, it's defeated (exact = bonus).
    4. Suffer damage — discard cards totaling enemy attack (minus shields). Can't cover it → you lose.
  • Enemy immunity: an enemy is immune to its own suit's power.
  • Combos: play 2–4 same-number cards if total ≤ 10 (all suit powers trigger). Aces pair with one other card.
  • Defeated enemies in hand: J=10, Q=15, K=20 (as attack or discard).
  • Solo Jester power: flip a set-aside Jester to discard your hand and refill to 8 (twice per game). 0 used = Gold, 1 = Silver, 2 = Bronze.
