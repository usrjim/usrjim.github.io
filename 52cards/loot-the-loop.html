Loot the Loop

Setup

  1. Standard 52-card deck + 1 Joker. Shuffle, hold face down.
  2. Leave space for 3 Notes cards and a Score Pile.

Rules

  • You're exploring a closed-loop temple (the deck). The top card = your location. Move forward by sending cards from top to bottom.
  • Aces = jewels (loot them). 2–10 = trinkets (loot them). Face cards = traps (avoid them). Joker = the exit.
  • Each turn, take exactly ONE of these actions:
    1. Look Around — flip the top two cards face up (only if the top card is face down). Your only option at the start.
    2. Explore — if either of the top two cards is a face-up trinket, choose its value and move that many cards to the bottom. Then:
      • Land on face-down → nothing.
      • Land on face-up trap → you lose.
      • Land on face-up jewel/trinket → move it to your Score Pile.
      • Land on face-up Joker → if you hold all 4 jewels, you win; otherwise nothing.
    3. Mark a Path — if the top card is a trinket and you have <3 Notes, move it to your Notes (max 3).
    4. Return — place a Note card face up on top of the deck.
  • You may look through face-up cards anytime; never face-down.
  • Lose if stuck with no safe move. Win by landing on the Joker with all four jewels scored. Score = total cards in your Score Pile (greed pays).
