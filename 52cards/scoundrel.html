Scoundrel

Setup

  1. Standard 52-card deck.
  2. Remove Jokers, red face cards (J/Q/K of ♥/♦), and red Aces (A♥, A♦) — 16 cards out, 36 remain.
  3. Shuffle the 36 and place face down on your left — the Dungeon.
  4. Note your starting Health: 20.

Rules

  • Clubs & Spades (26) = Monsters; value is ordered (A=14, K=13, … 2=2).
  • Diamonds (9) = Weapons; damage equals value. Weapons are binding — picking one up forces you to equip it and discard the previous.
  • Hearts (9) = Health Potions; add value to Health (max 20). Only one potion per turn; a second is discarded.
  • Each turn, flip 4 cards face up — a Room. You may avoid the Room (send all 4 to the bottom of the Dungeon), but never two Rooms in a row.
  • If you don't avoid, face 3 of the 4 cards, one at a time:
    • Weapon: equip it (discard previous weapon + its slain monsters).
    • Potion: heal, then discard.
    • Monster: fight barehanded (take full damage) or with your weapon (subtract weapon value from monster value; take the remainder). A weapon can only slay monsters ≤ the last one it slew.
  • The 4th card stays face up as the start of the next Room.
  • Game ends when Health hits 0 (lose) or the Dungeon is cleared (win).
