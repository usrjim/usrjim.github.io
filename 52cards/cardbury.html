Cardbury

Setup

  1. Shuffle a standard 52-card deck. Deal a face-down row of 4 cards (don't examine them). Place the deck to the left of the row.
  2. Draw 3 cards. If they all share a suit, shuffle them back and redraw until you have at least 2 suits in hand.

Rules

  • Goal: build 4 skyscrapers (one per suit, ascending A low → K high), of sizes 5 / 4 / 3 / 2 cards (left to right), without running out of deck.
  • Each turn has 4 steps:
    1. Pollution — if you hold 3+ of any suit, discard that set face-down as a pollution stack. If there are ever 6+ pollution stacks, or your hand empties from pollution, you lose.
    2. Population — for each "set in all 4 suits" OR each suit beyond the first with 2+ cards, move a deck card to the population stack. If the stack ever exceeds the value of the highest card in any skyscraper (K=13), you lose.
    3. Construction — add a hand card to a skyscraper (higher than the previous card; a new skyscraper needs a suit not yet built). If you start a new skyscraper, draw the card below it into your hand and loop back to step 1. If you can't play legally, dismantle: remove cards from a skyscraper until a legal placement exists (removed cards leave play).
    4. Supply — draw 2 cards. If the deck empties before all 4 skyscrapers are done, you lose.
