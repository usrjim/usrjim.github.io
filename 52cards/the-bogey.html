The Bogey

Setup

  1. Remove the Jokers from a standard 52-card deck. Shuffle.
  2. Deal yourself an opening hand of 5 cards. The board starts empty.

Rules

  • Goal: play all cards into as few columns as possible. Win thresholds: novice 12 · normal 11 · advanced 10 · legendary 9 · epic 8.
  • Columns: same suit, descending K→A (may skip values). Add a card to the bottom of a same-suit column of higher cards, or start a new column. Max 12 columns.
  • Play alternates between you and the Bogey, you go first.
  • Your turn: draw back to 5 cards. For each card in hand, decide to play it, discard it, or hold it for next turn.
  • The Bogey's turn: the next deck card is played — you choose where (add to a column or start a new one). If it can't be played legally, you lose.
  • When the deck runs out, shuffle the discards into a new deck.
  • Harder variant: hand size 4 instead of 5.
