Club Fight

Setup

  1. Choose difficulty: 1 Joker (easy) or 2 Jokers (hard).
  2. Separate the 13 clubs, shuffle → your draw pile. Draw 4 into your hand.
  3. Shuffle the remaining cards (39–40) → the grid deck.
  4. Deal 9 cards face up in a 3×3 grid. Leave space for discards.

Rules

  • Each turn: discard one club from your hand to clear grid cards via one of 6 actions, then refresh the grid (left→right, top row down) and draw back up to 4 clubs.
  • Values: A=1, J=11, Q=12, K=13. Jokers have no suit or value.
  • 13 turns max. Win by clearing the last grid cards with no grid deck left.

Actions

  • Always available:
    1. Match — clear all grid cards of the same value as your discarded club.
    2. Sum — clear any number of grid cards whose sum equals your club's value.
    3. Any — clear any one grid card (one of two actions that can clear a Joker).
  • Blockable (blocked when 3+ of a suit appear in the grid):
    1. Line — clear one row or column containing a card of the same value. (Blocked by 3+ ♦)
    2. Up/Down — discard 2–7♣ to clear all lower cards, or 7–Q♣ to clear all higher cards. (Blocked by 3+ ♠)
    3. Run — clear any number of grid cards that, with your club, form a sequential run (no wrap K→A). (Blocked by 3+ ♥)
